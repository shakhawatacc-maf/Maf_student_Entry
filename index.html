Aptitude exam for prospective students

A 20-minute, time-pressured assessment blending basic English, analytical ability, medium-level critical reasoning, accounting fundamentals and the use of AI tools at work.

  • 130 questions in 20 minutes. The 20 minutes is a shared budget. Each question also has its own time limit — submit your answer to move on, or it advances automatically when that limit is reached.
  • 2One direction only. After you answer, submit to get the next question. You cannot return to a previous question.
  • 3Random identity checks with a short timer will appear during the exam. Answer them from your own registration details.
  • 4Every paper is unique. The questions you receive and the order of the options differ from other candidates.
  • 5Stay on this screen. Leaving the exam tab to search elsewhere is recorded — and only wastes your limited time.
  • 6Pass mark is 50%. Passing candidates choose an office and receive interview details.
